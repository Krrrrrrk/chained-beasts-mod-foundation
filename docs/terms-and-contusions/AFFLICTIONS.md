# Potion afflictions

Afflictions come from normal potion choices, with the game's healing preserved. Effects use effective severity after suppression or reduction. Curing an affliction removes its future contribution and relevant pending state.

| Content / ID | Full scaling and limits | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| Leadfoot / 11010 | Effective severity 1/2/3: movement reduced 10/20/30%; native suppression/reduction/cure applies. | `[Leadfoot] EnableLeadfoot` |
| Lead Belly / 11011 | 1 skull(s): Stamina regeneration starts 0.25 seconds later after exertion. Recovery rate is unchanged. / 2 skull(s): Stamina regeneration starts 0.5 seconds later after exertion. Recovery rate is unchanged. / 3 skull(s): Stamina regeneration starts 0.75 seconds later after exertion. Recovery rate is unchanged. | `[Afflictions] EnableLeadBelly` |
| Brittle Guard / 11012 | 1 skull(s): Every 4 blocked shield hits consume one additional shield durability. / 2 skull(s): Every 3 blocked shield hits consume one additional shield durability. / 3 skull(s): Every 2 blocked shield hits consume one additional shield durability. | `[Afflictions] EnableBrittleGuard` |
| Shaky Resolve / 11013 | 1 skull(s): Actual enemy HP damage drains 5% maximum stamina, at most once per second. / 2 skull(s): Actual enemy HP damage drains 10% maximum stamina, at most once per second. / 3 skull(s): Actual enemy HP damage drains 15% maximum stamina, at most once per second. | `[Afflictions] EnableShakyResolve` |
| Tin Ear / 11014 | 1 skull(s): Your actions generate 15% less crowd favor. / 2 skull(s): Your actions generate 30% less crowd favor. / 3 skull(s): Your actions generate 45% less crowd favor. | `[Afflictions] EnableTinEar` |
| Burning Lungs / 11015 | 1 skull(s): Stamina regenerates 20% slower while moving; stationary recovery is normal. / 2 skull(s): Stamina regenerates 35% slower while moving; stationary recovery is normal. / 3 skull(s): Stamina regenerates 50% slower while moving; stationary recovery is normal. | `[Afflictions] EnableBurningLungs` |
| Heavy Landing / 11016 | 1 skull(s): After a dodge, move 20% slower for 0.75 seconds. The dodge itself is unchanged. / 2 skull(s): After a dodge, move 30% slower for 0.75 seconds. The dodge itself is unchanged. / 3 skull(s): After a dodge, move 40% slower for 0.75 seconds. The dodge itself is unchanged. | `[Afflictions] EnableHeavyLanding` |
| Loose Change / 11017 | 1 skull(s): Actual enemy HP damage loses 1 gold, at most once every three seconds, never below zero. / 2 skull(s): Actual enemy HP damage loses 2 gold, at most once every three seconds, never below zero. / 3 skull(s): Actual enemy HP damage loses 3 gold, at most once every three seconds, never below zero. | `[Afflictions] EnableLooseChange` |
| Cold Start / 11018 | 1 skull(s): Deal 10% less damage during the first ten seconds of each battle. / 2 skull(s): Deal 20% less damage during the first ten seconds of each battle. / 3 skull(s): Deal 30% less damage during the first ten seconds of each battle. | `[Afflictions] EnableColdStart` |
| Obsessive Focus / 11019 | 1 skull(s): After directly damaging an enemy, attacks against other enemies deal 15% less damage for three seconds. / 2 skull(s): After directly damaging an enemy, attacks against other enemies deal 25% less damage for three seconds. / 3 skull(s): After directly damaging an enemy, attacks against other enemies deal 35% less damage for three seconds. | `[Afflictions] EnableObsessiveFocus` |
| Overheating / 11020 | 1 skull(s): Consecutive melee attacks add 5% stamina cost per stack, up to three stacks. The first attack after two seconds idle has normal cost. / 2 skull(s): Consecutive melee attacks add 10% stamina cost per stack, up to three stacks. The first attack after two seconds idle has normal cost. / 3 skull(s): Consecutive melee attacks add 15% stamina cost per stack, up to three stacks. The first attack after two seconds idle has normal cost. | `[Afflictions] EnableOverheating` |

For implementation details, use the [content index](manifest.json), [shared mechanics and events](../foundation/MECHANICS.md) and [adding-content guide](../ADDING_CONTENT.md).

Normal offers are the default. Testing overrides require the developer-offer master and the relevant Force option. Actual gameplay, UI layout and save/resume interactions still need manual verification for your chosen combination.
